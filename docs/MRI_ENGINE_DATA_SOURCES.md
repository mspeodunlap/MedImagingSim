## Recommended acquisition order

1. **E0 baseline:** our own geometric/asymmetric and synthetic brain phantoms, with exact tissue values and known geometry. They make numerical tests reproducible without external access dependencies.
2. **First external brain:** evaluate BrainWeb fuzzy anatomy and one fully inspected MRiLab object. Compare importer and geometry behavior against synthetic truth; do not tune the engine merely to resemble their rendered images.
3. **First non-brain anatomy:** choose a bounded, rights-cleared TotalSegmentator MRI or AMOS subset after reviewing the actual release. Complete its tissue/species assignment and missing anatomy/background, then validate oblique sampling and field-specific contrast. Cardinality alone is not an anatomy-quality measure.
4. **Later MSK/cardiac/whole body:** MuscleMap, ACDC and XCAT/Visible Human require model-specific work. Cardiac gating, motion and flow await E3/E5 physics and trajectories; segmented ED/ES shapes alone are insufficient.
5. **Independent reconstruction evidence:** use approved fastMRI/OCMR subsets with their original acquisition metadata. Keep this corpus separate from phantom construction, parameter fitting and final validation cases. No benchmark data becomes a student-facing case automatically.

## Correct mask-to-phantom conversion

**Do not make Gaussian smoothing plus a weighted mean of T1/T2 the default conversion.** An organ label is not a homogeneous tissue type. Liver may contain blood/fat/lesion; bone labels do not separately describe cortical bone, marrow and surrounding tissue. Unknown/unlabeled space must stay explicit rather than silently becoming water, air or a default organ.

For non-exchanging sub-voxel compartments, preserve each tissue's fraction and properties and sum its **complex signal**:

\[
s_c(t)=\sum_{p,a}V_p f_{p,a}\rho_a C_c(\mathbf r_p)q_a(t)\,
\exp[-i2\pi\mathbf k(t)\cdot\mathbf r_p].
\]

This uses the main plan's normalized hybrid convention. Full Bloch states that already contain density/encoding use its alternative direct-state sum. In general, a sum of relaxation exponentials is not the exponential of an averaged T1/T2. Averaging time constants or rates is therefore not a general substitute for a compartment model. A fitted effective single tissue is a separately labeled approximation; fast exchange needs explicit exchange physics. Fractions alone do not create dephasing: spatial/frequency differences and acquisition evolution must be modeled. Take magnitude after complex mixing, and avoid applying partial-volume integration twice.

Planned importer procedure:

1. Record source record/version, checksum, asset terms, tissue/label dictionary, modality and spatial/time units. Read NIfTI/MINC/MAT affine, axis order, handedness and valid voxel support; preserve physical geometry, not array shape alone. MRiLab's documented Y×X×Z×species, seconds and Hz/T need explicit conversion to our contract.
2. Preserve existing fuzzy memberships. For hard labels, form tissue/compartment indicator functions and integrate/resample over the target support with a validated finite-volume or supersampled method. Interpolating integer label IDs is invalid; use nearest-neighbor if discrete labels must be resliced, or resample fractional channels with appropriate constraints.
3. Resolve overlapping anatomical labels using a documented ontology and precedence. Include background/unmodeled fractions. Require finite, nonnegative fractions and a partition sum within tolerance; do not independently smooth masks and accept overlapping probabilities or normalize away missing anatomy.
4. Treat optional smoothing as an explicit, testable boundary/PSF assumption. Record sigma in **millimeters**, boundary treatment and version; assess tissue volume, thin structures and landmark shifts. Genuine sharp boundaries can produce legitimate finite-k-space ringing. Arbitrary Gaussian blur may erase anatomy and is not required merely because labels are discrete.
5. Assign per-tissue/species rho/T1/T2/chemical shift and applicable B0/temperature/source uncertainties. Keep susceptibility/B0, B1+/receive coils, residual T2* dispersion and motion fields separate. An upsampled 1 mm segmentation does not acquire submillimeter anatomical truth.
6. Validate multi-compartment signal against analytic mixtures; check conservation/geometry and refinement convergence. Verify oblique boundaries, smallest supported structures, fat/water phase cancellation and RF-profile integration before converting the result into an accepted simulation asset.

A real raw dataset may not include an independent noise-only measurement, calibrated sensitivity maps, exact vendor processing or tissue truth. Reconstruction comparisons should state what metadata and reference images actually exist. Similar appearance is not proof of sequence-physics accuracy or absolute SNR calibration.

## E0 asset acceptance record

Before an asset enters implementation, record the exact immutable source/version and local hash; license/terms snapshot and intended permitted deployment; modality/sequence/field/age/pathology context; coordinate geometry and labels; complete versus unknown compartments; property-table provenance; interpolation and smoothing recipe; fields/motion actually available; importer/conservation/mixture/convergence tests; size/memory budget; and independent validation split. Keep raw, converted and derived artifacts traceable with separate hashes.

Current state for every candidate: **cataloged only, not adopted or imported**. Prefer small representative subsets and synthetic fixtures; do not download entire repositories or apply for access as part of this planning change. Recheck current owner terms and inspect asset-level files when E0 starts. Advanced anatomy and benchmark results remain future work.
