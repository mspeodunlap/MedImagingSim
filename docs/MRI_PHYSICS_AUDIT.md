## Corrections to avoid teaching the wrong relationship

- TE is excitation-to-echo time. A refocusing pulse at TE/2 does **not** undo intrinsic T2 relaxation. Use decay over the full TE; the PDF's contrast discussion on PDF pp. 38–39 describes TE/2 in a way that must not become the image equation.
- Increasing GRE flip angle does not always increase signal. The steady-state maximum depends on TR and tissue T1; over-flipping beyond the Ernst angle can reduce signal. Existing spoiled-GRE behavior was retained and tested against this maximum.
- Increasing TI does not always increase magnitude signal: it first approaches a tissue null and then rises. Null timing depends on tissue T1 and finite TR; no fixed TI is a universal FLAIR or STIR prescription.
- A long TR reduces T1 weighting; it does not guarantee complete recovery for every tissue. T1/T2/PD preset names supply starting values, not hard-coded contrast images.
- Receiver bandwidth, readout duration and minimum timing are related. A narrower bandwidth permits less noise and more fat displacement, but may make the requested TE/echo spacing infeasible. It does not automatically change a chosen, still-feasible TE.
- Spatial presaturation and chemical fat suppression are different mechanisms. A spatial band reduces magnetization in a location; it is not a fat-selective preparation.

| Receiver bandwidth | Noise, readout duration, minimum TE/spacing, fat displacement and package capacity. | Always Hz per readout pixel; no ±kHz ambiguity. 1.5 T is used only as the fixed frequency assumption, not a calibrated set of patient relaxation constants. |
| Plane, angle, stack center | Resample the same 3D anatomy in the prescribed location/orientation. | One-axis obliquity and through-plane translation; no hidden change in tissue contrast. |

## Quantitative model and reproducibility

SE amplitude uses density × (1 − exp(−TR/T1)) × exp(−TE/T2). IR replaces recovery with signed 1 − 2 exp(−TI/T1) + exp(−TR/T1). Spoiled GRE uses sin(alpha) × (1 − E1)/(1 − cos(alpha) E1) × exp(−TE/T2-star). These are idealized steady-state teaching equations. Signed tissue components are integrated before taking magnitude; applying absolute value independently to every IR tissue would prevent correct cancellation at mixed boundaries.

Sampling SNR, relative to 220 mm FOV / 128×128 / 5 mm / 250 Hz-per-pixel / one average / 2D, is voxel-volume ratio × square root of (encoded phase lines / 128 × averages × 250 / bandwidth × 3D partitions). Tissue signal/contrast is deliberately excluded from this indicator. Fixed image gain preserves contrast changes; images are not individually auto-normalized. Noise is added to real and imaginary channels before magnitude, producing a nonzero Rayleigh background and Rician magnitude signal. A deterministic seed makes controlled comparisons repeatable; there is no claim of a measured clinical SNR.

Readout duration is 1000 / bandwidth ms. Fat/water separation uses 3.5 ppm × 42.57747892 MHz/T × 1.5 T = approximately 223.53 Hz; displacement is this separation / Hz-per-pixel along readout. GRE additionally preserves fat/water phase at TE, including mixed-voxel cancellation. A single fat resonance is assumed; no multi-peak Dixon reconstruction.

FSE applies tissue-specific Fourier weighting along the full encoded phase dimension. Central frequency bands are assigned to the first echo at effective TE; successively more peripheral bands use later echoes at TE + echo index × spacing. Each tissue's additional attenuation is exp(−echo index × spacing/T2). A mixed-radix DFT and inverse DFT reconstruct the modulated tissue components before complex summation/noise/magnitude. This implements a specified center-first educational echo order, not all clinical FSE ordering strategies. The strongest changes affect phase detail; ETL and spacing cannot be described merely as time controls anymore.

Model timing reserves 4 ms overhead plus the last echo and half the readout for a slice slot. IR additionally reserves TI before the package's slice slots. The number of 2D slices per package is floor(available TR / slot), at least one; packages are ceil(slices / capacity). Time is ceil(encoded phase lines / ETL) × TR × averages × packages for 2D, or × partitions for 3D GRE. FSE ETL is otherwise one. These scheduling/overhead choices are original explicit teaching assumptions, not measured scanner limits. Minimum TE is 2 + half readout for GRE or 4 + full readout for refocused sequences; FSE spacing also accommodates 4 + readout and TE must be at least one spacing. Preparation/full train/readout must fit TR. Impossible requests are rejected, never silently clamped.

The synthetic tissue constants are versioned fixture values, not patient-specific relaxation measurements. The fixed field assumption does not justify applying 1.5 T clinical diagnostic accuracy to them. Image-model version, voxel dimensions, line count, noise scale, readout, displacement, packages, duration and echo-order description are saved with every new acquisition. Existing pixels and engine metadata are preserved; earlier series show an earlier-model notice. Existing library versions are not overwritten. Reading an old FSE definition supplies the documented 10 ms default; loading/editing creates the normal independent case copy/new immutable version. Old timings that fail the corrected checks must be revised explicitly in the full editor.

## Verification and limits

Automated checks cover analytic TE/TR ratios, preset independence, GRE Ernst-angle behavior, finite-TR IR null/sign, voxel/phase/frequency/averaging/bandwidth/3D SNR ratios, readout and displacement units, 2D packaging/3D timing, invalid combinations and duplicate fields, Fourier DC/frequency response across 64/96/128 and prime-length 137 encodings on both axes, rendered magnitude-noise statistics, FSE image changes/sharpness, actual phantom fat displacement rotating with readout, and read-time compatibility without source mutation. Existing API/ownership, default/personal version, review-lock and concurrent-user suites also run.

A disposable fictional-data browser exercise verifies live metrics, echo-spacing enablement, bandwidth timing errors, acquisition, stored model evidence, personal/default controls and generated guide delivery. Exact final counts/results and classroom timings are recorded in the milestone handoff. The richer voxel model costs more CPU than v2; local classroom timing is observational and not a ThinkPad service-level guarantee.

Remaining limitations include exact Bloch/EPG evolution, realistic head anatomy, RF slice/slab profiles and cross-talk, FSE fat J-coupling and variable refocusing, spatial field/susceptibility maps, coil sensitivity/reconstruction, Gibbs ringing in ordinary non-FSE images, motion/flow/gating, SAR and hardware constraints. DWI/ADC, EPI/spiral trajectories, perfusion/contrast agents, ASL, TOF/phase-contrast flow, SWI, balanced SSFP, Dixon/chemical fat suppression, quantitative mapping, neuromelanin and MT remain reference-only. Their specialized parameters are not offered as fake active acquisition controls. Full Radiopaedia linked-page review, educator comparison with clinical consoles, ThinkPad/LAN capacity and full print-layout verification remain pending.
